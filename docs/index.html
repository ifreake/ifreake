01 / A LITTLE INTRO

Curious by nature.
Builder by choice.

I enjoy exploring the space where software meets hardware.

My interests move between programming, embedded systems, IoT, and practical development. I like understanding how things work, experimenting with new ideas, and turning those ideas into projects that do something useful.

Sometimes that means writing a tool in Python. Sometimes it means debugging C++ firmware, wiring up a display, or making a microcontroller communicate with a local interface. The mix of logic, experimentation, and real-world feedback is what keeps me motivated.
